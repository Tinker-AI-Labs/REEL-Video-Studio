Quality Checklist
One primary action + one camera move per clip — chaining events causes morphing.
Aim for 40–120 words; describe what should be there, never what shouldn't.
Put the camera move and subject first — models weight early words heavily.
Reuse the same reference image + locked traits for every clip of a character.
Light with cheap models, finalize with Veo / Sora / Aleph.
Action reading slow? Cut to 4–6s, use words like rapid / explosive / snap, and choreograph one countable exchange per clip.
